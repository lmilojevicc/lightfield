## Paper Shaders — @paper-design/shaders 0.0.80 (Apache-2.0)

Powered by Paper Shaders: https://shaders.paper.design
Source: https://github.com/paper-design/shaders/tree/60467401863c1917dd02016d0c1ff2f791d0b3c8
Pinned npm package: @paper-design/shaders 0.0.80. Copyright 2026 Paper.

Nine original procedural and seven image-filter fragment shaders and the bundled
128×128 noise PNG are used unchanged; no demo photographs, React runtime or remote
scripts are included. Meridian supplies its own optional local-file image UI.
The copied `src/backgrounds/paper/vertex-shader.ts` is unchanged upstream source.
`src/backgrounds/paper/shader-mount.ts` is a modified upstream mount: synchronous
first-paint readiness and exceptions, runtime draw/upload/context-loss callbacks,
1–1.5 DPR maximum with a 2,073,600-pixel budget, explicit vertex-buffer/context release,
uniform/image-reference cleanup, full-source image cache keys, and new image-uniform
location discovery. An opt-in scalar sizing hook shares the mount clock/draw for
Meridian's Radial Gradient drift (±1% viewport, 45s/60s) and breathing (±2%, 50s),
with smooth bound tapering; the official static radial fragment, vertex source,
palette mapping and frame-zero composition remain unchanged.
The image integration also exposes the cached owning-context
texture limit and rejects oversized uploads before allocation; inactive uniforms
are silently skipped rather than emitting repeated warnings. Readiness includes framebuffer completeness and GL errors, not
pixel brightness or alpha. GL errors are checked on initialization and invalidated
(uniform/resize) paints, avoiding a synchronous query on every animation tick.
Official React-wrapper defaults and uniform mappings are adapted in definitions.ts
and nine effect modules. Theme palette substitution, bounded controls, opacity/CSS
filters and reduced-motion/visibility integration are Meridian changes, not Paper presets.
Seven image-filter prop mappings/defaults, scoped validation, deferred bounded image
uploads and cancellable embedded-noise ownership live under paper/images. Only
semantic paper/ink defaults follow the theme; CMYK inks and image algorithms retain
color. Previews use Meridian-owned geometric art with capture-only grayscale.
All vertex/fragment algorithms remain unchanged. See docs/paper-backgrounds.md and
docs/paper-image-filters.md.
